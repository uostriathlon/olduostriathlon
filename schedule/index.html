    Typical Training Schedule

    This is the updated weekly training schedule for the 24/25 timetable.  The training is scheduled to fit around university timetables of lectures 9am-5pm with Wednesday afternoons usually being free of lectures. Each session is optional and you are able to pick those that are appropriate for you. Of course we encourage you to come to them all if you can!

    The University of Sheffield Triathlon Club is partly funded by The Alumni Foundation.

    The University of Sheffield Triathlon Club is part of the University of Sheffield's Sports Department.
