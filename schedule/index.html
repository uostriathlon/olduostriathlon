Typical Training Schedule

Updated weekly training schedule for the 24/25 timetable. Sessions are scheduled around lectures (9am–5pm) with Wednesday afternoons usually free. Every session is optional – pick what suits you.

DaySessionNotes
MondaySwim / RunAbility groups
TuesdayCycle (outdoor or spin)Peak District or indoor
WednesdaySwim + S&CAfternoon free for many
ThursdayRunMultiple pace groups
FridaySwim / Easy spinRecovery options
WeekendLong ride / long runSocial rides in the Peaks

Exact times and meeting points are posted on the Facebook group and Instagram each week. Club bikes are available to borrow.
