Races

Throughout the academic year the University of Sheffield Triathlon Club participates in a wide variety of races, both locally and nationwide BUCS events.

Typical racing calendar

  • Give It A Go Aquathlon – end of September / beginning of October
  • Give It A Go Duathlon – beginning of October
  • BUCS Duathlon – End of November
  • Birmingham Aquathlon – End of February
  • Varsity Sprint Triathlon – End of March
  • BUCS Standard Triathlon – Mid May

We encourage as many members as possible to take part and organise transport for you and your bikes. Give It A Go events are only a couple of pounds to enter. Most other races are heavily subsidised by the club.

We also promote local races such as the Hathersage Aquathlon Series, Leeds Abbey Dash 10k, Percy Pud 10k, Sheffield Half Marathon and local sprint triathlons.
