Give It A Go Duathlon

Welcome to the University of Sheffield Triathlon Club’s Give it a Go Duathlon!

If you’re looking to try something brand new, meet some awesome friendly people, or improve an existing talent, our duathlon is the event for you! Aimed at both the beginner and more experienced sportsman/woman – however confident you feel, come and give it a go.

Entry

Entry costs only £2 – please bring this on the day and fill out the race briefing, sign-up slots and health declaration forms in advance.

The Race

There will be both a long and a short course for all abilities. Run–bike–run held on the outskirts of the beautiful Peak District.

Getting Here

  • By bike: We cycle you to the edge of the Peak District (~7 km) from The Edge (Endcliffe student village).
  • By bus: Number 51 towards Lodge Moor, last stop, then ~300 m walk to the Sportsman Lodge Moor.
  • By car: Field behind the Sportsman Lodge Moor, 569 Redmires Rd, Sheffield S10 4LJ. Use the free car park just after the pub – not the pub car park.

You will need to bring

  • A helmet – No helmet, no race
  • U-card (if you have one)
  • Entry fee

YOU DON’T NEED A BIKE! Message us on Facebook or Instagram and we can arrange a club bike for you.

After the race we usually have a drink at the Sportsman Lodge Moor – a good chance to meet everyone.
