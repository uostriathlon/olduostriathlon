Membership

We are open to ALL ABILITIES. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced triathlete, you are welcome.

At the start of the academic year we offer two weeks of free training plus our popular Give It A Go events so you can try the club before joining.

Membership gives you access to all formal training sessions, club kit, race support, socials, the training holiday and member discounts with our sponsors.

For the latest membership fees and how to join, please check our Instagram @sheffunitri or email triathlon@sheffield.ac.uk.
