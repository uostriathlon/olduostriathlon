Give It A Go Aquathlon

Welcome to the University of Sheffield Triathlon Club’s Give it a Go Aquathlon!

If you’re looking to try something brand new, meet some awesome friendly people, or improve an existing talent, our Aquathlon is the event for you!

The Aquathlon consists of a Swim then a Run. Whether you are a previous swimmer or runner, this event is aimed at both the beginner and more experienced sportsperson with two different distances. No matter how confident you feel please come and have a go!

Entry

Entry costs only £1. Bring this on the day and fill out the Race Briefing, Sign-up slots and Health Declaration forms in advance.

The Race

There will be both long and short distance options available for both the swim (200/400 m) and run (2/3 km). Staggered starts may be used depending on numbers and any distancing requirements.

Getting Here

Our Give it a Go Aquathlon begins at the Goodwin Sports Centre.

What to bring

  • Swimming kit – come dressed with your swimming costume under your clothes
  • Running kit
  • U-card (if you have one)
  • £1 entry

If you have any questions please get in touch via Instagram or Facebook!
