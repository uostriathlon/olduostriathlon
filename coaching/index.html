Coaching

Our training sessions are led by experienced committee members and coaches who structure sessions so that everyone benefits – from complete beginners to seasoned triathletes.

We run multiple ability groups for swim, bike and run so you can train at the right intensity and progress at your own pace. Strength & Conditioning sessions complement the three disciplines.

If you have specific goals or need advice on technique, equipment or racing, speak to any of the training officers or the captain – we are always happy to help.
