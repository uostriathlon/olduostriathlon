    Charity Events

    ​​

    ​

    ​

    In our first Charity Week (27th April - 3rd May) Lauren ran our first charity event. The aim was to run, cycle or swim the distance to the cancelled training holiday in one week: 1992km to Alcudia. 

    ​

    The week was a huge success with 40 people (club members and non-club members) taking part and travelling just over 4000km in total! As a club we raised an amazing £515 for the chosen charity: Sheffield Hospitals Charity, to support our local hospitals in the difficult times they are facing. 

    ​

    To show off the hard work of the people in the club, a short video was made to show the achievement!

    Each year the club holds lots of charity events, such as a 24 hour relay and a whole week of charity fundraising. The funds go to a charity voted by the members!

    ​

    This year we’re really keen to get more involved in fundraising for charity and are excited to see what new events and ideas we can come up with!
