Charity Events

In our first Charity Week Lauren ran our first charity event. The aim was to run, cycle or swim the distance to the cancelled training holiday in one week: 1992 km to Alcudia.

The week was a huge success with 40 people (club members and non-club members) taking part and travelling just over 4000 km in total! As a club we raised an amazing £515 for the chosen charity: Sheffield Hospitals Charity, to support our local hospitals in the difficult times they are facing.

Each year the club holds lots of charity events, such as a 24 hour relay and a whole week of charity fundraising. The funds go to a charity voted by the members!

This year we’re really keen to get more involved in fundraising for charity and are excited to see what new events and ideas we can come up with!
