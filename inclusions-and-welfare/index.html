Inclusions and Welfare

As a club, we recognise that we have a diverse group of athletes, and we value and embrace the diversity of all of our members. We strive to be inclusive and mindful in our training sessions, races, socials and ethos. In doing so, we aim to establish and maintain an environment that fosters equitable participation for club members regardless of ability or background.

Here is a message from our Charity and Welfare Officer:

As wellbeing officer my role is to offer support and encouragement to both new and old members alike, as well as answer any tri-related questions (no question is too silly!)

If you ever need to talk or have a concern, please reach out to the Inclusions / Welfare officer or any committee member, or email triathlon@sheffield.ac.uk.
